# 03 — Cards, Status, Relics

## Card types

### ATTACK
Immediate damage.

### TACTIC
Buff/debuff/card manipulation.

### MODULE
Tồn tại trên battlefield và tạo rule/passive.

### DRONE / SUMMON
Tạo entity có HP/behavior, enemy có thể target.

### FIELD
Thay đổi rule battlefield.

## STONE identity

Stable → Armor → Setup → Fracture → Heavy Hit.

Keywords:
- Armor
- Fortify
- Fracture
- Crystal
- Heavy
- Counter

Example cards:

### Rock Cannon
Energy 1 / Mass 1  
Deal `7 + Arsenal` damage.

### Layered Plating
Energy 1 / Mass 1  
Gain `7 + Engineering` Shield.

### Crystal Resonator
Energy 1 / Mass 2 / Module  
Every 3rd Stone card this turn: gain +2 Arsenal until end of turn.

### Seismic Cannon
Energy 2 / Mass 2  
Deal 14. If target has Fracture, +8.

### Ore Drone
Energy 2 / Mass 2  
Summon Ore Drone. Every 2 turns it grants 5 Armor.

## PLANT identity

Plant → Grow → Poison → Multiply / Sustain.

Keywords:
- Poison
- Growth
- Regen
- Seed
- Root
- Bloom
- Summon

### Toxic Spore
Energy 1 / Mass 1  
Apply Poison 3.

### Germinate
Energy 1 / Mass 1  
Create Seed. After 2 turns, Seed becomes Bloom.

### Bloom
Passive/Module  
Plant effects gain improved scaling while active.

### Leech Vine
Energy 2 / Mass 2  
Deal 5. Repair Hull based on Poison on target, capped.

### Spore Colony
Module  
Enemy dies while Poisoned → spread Poison to remaining enemies.

## Status ideas

### Corrosion
Deals direct Hull damage on tick; bypass behavior must be explicit.

### Jam
Card locked X turns.

### Suppression
Specified card type costs +1 Energy temporarily.

### Fracture
Next qualifying physical/heavy attack gains bonus.

### Root
Card stays in hand but cannot be used, reducing available hand space.

### Junk
Debris/Spore/Malware inserted into deck/discard depending effect.

### Weakness
Temporary Arsenal penalty.

### System Failure
Temporary Engineering penalty.

## Relics

Relics nên đổi rule, không chỉ +5%.

Examples:

### Stoneheart Reactor
First Mass 2+ card each turn costs 1 less Energy.

### Emergency Seal
Once per battle, when Hull drops below threshold, repair Hull.

### Mycelium Core
First time applying Poison each turn, add extra Poison.

### Overloaded Cargo Rack
+3 Cargo; -10 Max Hull.

## Duplicate cards

Duplicate không thành rác.

Duplicate → Planet Knowledge.

Knowledge dùng để:
- targeted craft,
- reroll pack,
- unlock research,
- pity/progression.
