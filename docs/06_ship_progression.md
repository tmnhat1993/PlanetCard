## Why only 4 scaling stats

Đủ để tạo build identity mà không biến deckbuilder thành spreadsheet RPG.

### Arsenal
Direct damage, missile, burn/heavy offense.

### Engineering
Shield, repair, armor, module stability.

### Science
Poison, corrosion, debuff, field/environment.

### Command
Drone, summon, draw, hand manipulation.

## Starting deck target

3 × Cannon Shot — Mass 1  
3 × Plating — Mass 1  
1 × Targeting — Mass 1  
1 × Fortify — Mass 1  
1 × Heavy Cannon — Mass 2

Total: 9 cards / 10 Mass.

## Deck rules for testing

- Minimum card count: 8.
- Soft/initial maximum: 20.
- Hard capacity primarily controlled by Mass.

Compare:
- 14 cards / 18 Mass = flexible.
- 9 cards / 18 Mass = compact/consistent/heavy.

Đây là deliberate deckbuilding decision.
