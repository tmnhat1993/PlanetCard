# PixelLab Prompt Template

Combine:
GLOBAL ART BIBLE
+ FACTION ART BIBLE
+ ASSET BRIEF

Then flatten into one concise generation description.

Template:

`[Asset name], [role] for a retro sci-fi pixel art JRPG card battler. [silhouette]. [materials/body]. [gameplay readability]. [faction shape language]. [palette direction]. Low top-down three-quarter view, upper-left lighting, chunky readable pixel clusters, selective outline, limited material shading, transparent background, no tiny noisy details.`

Do not rely on one huge permanent prompt. Keep:
- global rules,
- faction rules,
- asset-specific rules
as separate source documents and assemble them.
