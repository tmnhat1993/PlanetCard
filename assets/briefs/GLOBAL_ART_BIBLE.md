# Global Art Bible

STYLE:
Retro sci-fi pixel art JRPG with modern readable UI scale.

PIXEL LANGUAGE:
- chunky readable clusters
- strong silhouettes
- selective outline
- limited material shading
- no smooth gradients
- no anti-aliased sprite edges
- avoid tiny noisy pixels

CAMERA:
Low top-down 3/4 for battlefield entities unless asset brief says otherwise.

LIGHT:
Default upper-left.

READABILITY:
Asset must remain identifiable at intended in-game size.

MOOD:
Strange planetary civilizations, adventurous rather than grimdark.

DO NOT:
- photorealism
- painterly smoothing
- overly dense micro-detail
- glossy mobile-game 3D render
- fake text inside generated UI assets
