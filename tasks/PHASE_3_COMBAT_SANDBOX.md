# PHASE 3 — Combat Sandbox

## Objective

Kiểm chứng deck preparation và combat identity trước khi mở rộng meta loop.

## Content target

- 20–25 cards.
- 6 enemy archetypes.
- 5–8 encounters.
- 1 boss.
- 1 summon.
- 1 module.
- 2–3 relics.
- 3 deck presets.
- 3-battle debug expedition.

## Required deck approaches

- STONE Heavy/Fracture payoff.
- STONE Defense/Module engine.
- Tempo/hybrid test deck dùng primitive hiện có.

## Art budget

- Player ship candidate.
- 3 basic enemy silhouettes.
- Elite/boss placeholder or candidate.
- Card frames.
- Core status/intent icons.
- Simple combat background.

Chạy PixelLab theo `docs/17_pixellab_production_workflow.md`. Không làm full card illustration batch.

## Exit gate

- Ít nhất 3 deck approaches chơi khác nhau.
- Encounter composition khiến player muốn đổi 2–4 cards.
- Boss có ít nhất 2 viable counter approaches.
- Normal encounter gần target 4–7 turns sau first tuning pass.
- Intent/status/output explanation readable.
- Same seed reproducible.
- Team quyết định `GO`, `ITERATE`, hoặc `STOP` trước Base integration.
