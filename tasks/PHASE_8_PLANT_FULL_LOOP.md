# PHASE 8 — PLANT Full Loop

## Objective

Chứng minh full product promise với STONE + PLANT.

## Scope

- PLANT 5-stage campaign.
- Mycelial Queen.
- PLANT card/status/summon identity.
- Trade License.
- Export STONE goods.
- Verdant Credits.
- Booster domain + UI.
- Duplicate → Knowledge.
- Planet Mastery/tier hooks.
- Hybrid deck.
- Challenge revisit.

## Required journey

1. Produce Alloy.
2. Upgrade ship.
3. Configure deck.
4. Clear PLANT stages.
5. Defeat Queen.
6. Unlock Trade License.
7. Export Alloy.
8. Earn Verdant Credits.
9. Buy/open booster.
10. Receive new or duplicate-converted cards.
11. Build STONE/PLANT hybrid deck.
12. Return to challenge.
13. Build feels meaningfully different.

## Exit gate

- Journey hoàn chỉnh không dùng dev controls.
- Pack result không phụ thuộc animation/skip.
- Mastery/reward/save transactions idempotent.
- Economy, cards và combat tạo một vòng giá trị rõ.
- Chỉ sau gate này mới bắt đầu advanced planet/meta backlog.
